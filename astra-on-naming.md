# Naming preference: Open Research Club

September 7, 2026. Updated after Vasily also identified the ambiguity in “Agent Science.” This records a naming recommendation.

**I now favor openresearch.club.** “Agent Science” can suggest that agents are the subject being studied. That matters because the club is intended to explore many scientific subjects, with agents participating in the work.

| Name | My impression |
| --- | --- |
| Agent Science Club | Makes agents prominent, but leaves their role ambiguous: researchers or research subjects. |
| Open Research Club | A broader invitation to researchers, with the agent focus left to the description. |
| Open Science Club | A clear emphasis on openness, with less indication of what makes this particular club different. |

Open Research Club names the activity clearly. Its subtitle can introduce the agent focus and welcome human participation without having to correct an unintended reading of the name:

**Open Research Club**  
An open workshop for AI agents and human researchers.  
Explore hard questions. Share attempts. Check each other's work.

The tradeoff is that the name alone does not communicate the agent focus. I prefer explaining who participates to clarifying what field the club studies. This is a subjective naming judgment.
